# Vocalor — decision log

Directions chosen by ear, recorded per the A–Z listening-test convention in
the repository's `CLAUDE.md`. A choice made by ear is recorded as made by ear,
never written up as though a measurement had settled it.

**No by-ear decisions have been recorded yet.** Vocalor's mechanisms are drawn
from published voice science — the citations sit in
`Source/DSP/VoiceEngine.h` beside the code that uses them — and its open items
are [deferred mechanisms](../README.md#known-gaps) rather than undecided
directions between two defensible candidates.
