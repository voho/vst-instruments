# Ghostar — decision log

Directions chosen by ear, recorded per the A–Z listening-test convention in
the repository's `CLAUDE.md`. A choice made by ear is recorded as made by ear,
and never written up as though a measurement had settled it.

**No by-ear decisions have been recorded yet.** Every law the engine applies
is anchored in a primary document or derived from one; the constants that
remain voiced are listed under [known gaps](../README.md#known-gaps) with the
measurement that would close each. Those are measurement questions, not
questions a listening test may answer — a listening test chooses between
candidates that are each already defensible on their own physics, and none of
the open items here has two such candidates.
