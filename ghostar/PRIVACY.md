# Privacy notice

Effective: 21 August 2026

Ghostar is an offline audio product. As shipped, it has no accounts,
advertising, telemetry, analytics, automatic update checks, network-based
licensing, or built-in crash-report upload. The plug-ins and standalone
application do not intentionally send audio, MIDI, parameter state, device
identifiers, or usage data anywhere.

Audio and MIDI are processed on the user's machine. A DAW may store Ghostar's
parameter state in its own project or preset files under the DAW's terms. The
standalone application uses the audio and MIDI devices the user selects and
may retain those choices in its platform's application-support location
(for example `~/Library/Application Support/Ghostar.settings` on macOS). Ghostar
does not upload those files or device settings.

The operating system and a host application may perform plug-in validation,
security checks, logging, backups, or crash reporting under their own
settings and privacy terms. In particular, macOS may contact Apple when
verifying a notarized installer or application. Purchases, downloads, website
visits, and payment processing are governed by the privacy terms of the
service through which they occur.

If a user contacts support, the publisher receives the information they
choose to send, such as an email address, diagnostic details, or attachments.
It is used only to answer the request, investigate the issue, meet legal
obligations, and protect the service. Users should not send confidential
projects, credentials, or recordings unless they are necessary and safe to
share.
