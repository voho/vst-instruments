# YouKnow factory-preset gain audit

Generated by `YouKnowAuditFactoryPresets` from the immutable factory tone-memory states and their product controls. The eighteen tone bytes are the hardware's and are never touched, and there is no per-preset corrective gain, EQ or limiting anywhere in the signal path. What each preset does carry is a VR1 output shaft position, exactly as a player would set one; those positions are listed in `Source/DSP/YouKnowPresets.cpp` and are attenuation only.

This run enforces two absolute contracts and fails if either is broken: no preset may peak above -1.0 dBFS, and none may exceed -28.5 dBFS gated RMS. Levels below those remain measurements rather than targets; source-level fidelity is documented separately in Docs/hardware-validation.md.

- Engine: 48 kHz, quality 4x.
- Analysis override: Chorus Noise = 0 so the signal metrics do not measure the continuously running BBD hiss.
- Score: 250 ms preroll; notes 36, 48, 60 and 72, each held 4 s and released 1 s; then 48/55/60/64/67/72 held 4 s and released 2 s. All note velocities are 1.0. Long release tails deliberately overlap the following events, so this is a musical/headroom stress score rather than an isolated control-to-gain calibration.
- RMS: 400 ms stereo windows at 100 ms hops. The relative-gated RMS is the energy mean of windows at or above max RMS -20 dB, with a -140 dBFS floor. Crest is sample peak / gated RMS.
- Preview delivery: exact product controls including Chorus Noise; one common non-boosting gain of 1.000000 (0.00 dB) for every file; no per-file normalisation. A raised-cosine 50 ms file-end fade prevents an audition-player stop click and is not part of the synth render.

Machine-readable values and exact 18-byte tone hex are in `metrics.csv`.

The full-bank run was followed by a targeted full-score rerender of A51 after its output trim changed from 0.660 to 0.645. All 128 measured rows meet both limits; the other 127 rows and the corpus median are unchanged.
