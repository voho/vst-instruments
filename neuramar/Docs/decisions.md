# Neuramar — decision log

Directions chosen by ear, recorded per the A–Z listening-test convention in
the repository's `CLAUDE.md`. A choice made by ear is recorded as made by ear,
never written up as though a measurement had settled it.

**No by-ear decisions have been recorded yet.** Neuramar's engine changes are
scored by the regression benchmark described under
[measured resynthesis quality](../README.md#measured-resynthesis-quality), and
the papers each branch follows are cited in `Source/DSP/NeuralModel.h`. Where
that benchmark records a trade rather than an improvement — the joint solve
buying tonal accuracy and spending some on transient material — the trade is
stated, not resolved by preference.
