# Privacy notice

Effective: 17 August 2026

YouKnow106 is an offline audio product from Protocodus. As shipped, it has no
accounts, advertising, telemetry, analytics, automatic update checks,
network-based licensing, or built-in crash-report upload. The plug-ins and
standalone application do not intentionally send audio, MIDI, presets, device
identifiers, or usage data to Protocodus.

Audio and MIDI are processed on the user's Mac. A DAW may store YouKnow106
parameter state in its project or preset files. LOAD reads a `.syx` file chosen
by the user, and SAVE writes a `.syx` file to a user-selected location. The
standalone application uses the audio and MIDI devices selected by the user.
It may retain those standalone device choices in
`~/Library/Application Support/YouKnow106.settings`. YouKnow106 does not upload
those files or device settings.

macOS and a host application may perform plug-in validation, security checks,
logging, backups, or crash reporting under their own settings and privacy
terms. In particular, macOS may contact Apple when verifying the notarized
installer or application. Purchases, downloads, website visits, and payment
processing are governed by the privacy terms of the service through which they
occur.

If a user contacts support, Protocodus receives the information they choose to
send, such as an email address, diagnostic details, or attachments. It is used
only to answer the request, investigate the issue, meet legal obligations, and
protect the service. Users should not send confidential projects, credentials,
or recordings unless they are necessary and safe to share.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to
[protocodus@proton.me](mailto:protocodus@proton.me). Product and company
information is available at [protocodus.cz](https://protocodus.cz). An
updated notice will accompany a release if YouKnow106's data practices change.
