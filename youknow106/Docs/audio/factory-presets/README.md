# YouKnow106 factory-preset gain audit

Generated by `YouKnow106AuditFactoryPresets` from the immutable factory tone-memory states and their product controls. This is a measurement report, not an equal-loudness target: overloads and level differences are warnings only, and no preset bytes or per-preset gains are changed.

- Engine: 48 kHz, HQ enabled.
- Analysis override: Chorus Noise = 0 so the signal metrics do not measure the continuously running BBD hiss.
- Score: 250 ms preroll; notes 36, 48, 60 and 72, each held 4 s and released 1 s; then 48/55/60/64/67/72 held 4 s and released 2 s. All note velocities are 1.0. Long release tails deliberately overlap the following events, so this is a musical/headroom stress score rather than an isolated control-to-gain calibration.
- RMS: 400 ms stereo windows at 100 ms hops. The relative-gated RMS is the energy mean of windows at or above max RMS -20 dB, with a -140 dBFS floor. Crest is sample peak / gated RMS.
- Preview delivery: exact product controls including Chorus Noise; one common non-boosting gain of 0.298320 (-10.51 dB) for every file; no per-file normalisation. A raised-cosine 50 ms file-end fade prevents an audition-player stop click and is not part of the synth render.

Machine-readable values and exact 18-byte tone hex are in `metrics.csv`.
